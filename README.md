

# EXP 5: COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS OF DIFFERENT TYPES OF PROMPTING PATTERNS AND EXPLAIN WITH VARIOUS TEST SCENARIOS

# Aim: To test and compare how different pattern models respond to various prompts (broad or unstructured) versus basic prompts (clearer and more refined) across multiple scenarios.  Analyze the quality, accuracy, and depth of the generated responses 

# Explanation: 
Basic Prompt: A simple direct instruction given to the AI without extra guidance.
Zero-shot Prompt: A prompt where the AI performs a task without any prior examples.
Few-shot Prompt: A prompt that includes a few examples to guide the AI in generating better responses.
Chain-of-Thought Prompt: A prompt that encourages the AI to explain its reasoning step by step before giving the final answer

# Test scenarios:
## Activity 1 – Content Summarization
### Basic Prompt:
“Summarize the importance of renewable energy.”
### Output:
Renewable energy is important because it reduces pollution, saves natural resources, and helps fight climate change. Sources like solar and wind energy are sustainable and environmentally friendly.

### Zero-shot Prompt:
“Explain the importance of renewable energy in about 100 words.”
### Output
Renewable energy plays a vital role in protecting the environment and ensuring sustainable development. Energy sources such as solar, wind, and hydroelectric power reduce dependence on fossil fuels, lowering greenhouse gas emissions and air pollution. Renewable resources are naturally replenished and help conserve limited natural resources for future generations. They also promote energy security and create employment opportunities in green industries. Using renewable energy can slow climate change and support a cleaner and healthier planet.

### Few-shot Prompt
“Summarize the topic Renewable Energy in a clear academic style.
### Output
Renewable energy refers to energy generated from natural resources such as sunlight, wind, and water. It helps reduce environmental pollution, decreases dependence on fossil fuels, and supports sustainable economic growth. Renewable energy technologies are essential for addressing global climate change and ensuring long-term energy security.

### Chain-of-Thought Prompt
“Explain step by step why renewable energy is important. Include environmental, economic, and social benefits before giving the final conclusion.”
### Output
Renewable energy sources such as solar and wind power do not release large amounts of harmful gases into the atmosphere. This helps reduce pollution and global warming.
Fossil fuels are limited resources, whereas renewable energy sources are naturally replenished. This ensures long-term energy availability.
Renewable energy industries create jobs in manufacturing, installation, and maintenance sectors, improving economic growth.
Cleaner energy improves public health by reducing respiratory diseases caused by pollution.
Countries using renewable energy become less dependent on imported fuels, improving energy security.
Final Conclusion: Renewable energy is important because it protects the environment, supports economic development, improves public health, and ensures a sustainable future.

### Activity 2 – Creative Writing
### Basic Prompt
“Write a short story about a robot.”
### Output
A robot named Robo lived in a small town and helped people with daily tasks. Everyone appreciated its kindness and intelligence.

### Zero-shot Prompt
“Write a short story in 120 words about a helpful robot living in a village.”
### Output
A robot named Alpha lived in a quiet village surrounded by green fields. Every morning, Alpha helped farmers water crops and repair broken tools. The villagers initially feared the robot, but they slowly realized its kindness and honesty. One day, a heavy storm damaged many homes. Alpha worked day and night to rescue people and rebuild the village. After the disaster, the villagers celebrated Alpha as their hero. From then on, the robot became an important member of the community, proving that technology can support humanity when used wisely.

### Few-shot Prompt
### Prompt
“Example 1:
Theme: Friendship
Story: Two friends worked together and solved problems with teamwork.

Example 2:
Theme: Courage
Story: A young girl overcame her fear to save her pet.

Now write a story for:
Theme: Helpful Robot”

### Output
A robot named Neo was designed to assist elderly people in a city. Every day, Neo reminded them to take medicine, cleaned their homes, and kept them company. One winter night, the electricity failed across the city. Neo used its backup power to help people stay safe and warm until help arrived. The residents realized that kindness and care are more important than whether someone is human or machine.

### Chain-of-Thought Prompt
“Think step by step about the setting, characters, conflict, and solution before writing a short story about a helpful robot.”
### Output
Setting: A peaceful farming village.
Character: A robot named Max designed to help people.
Conflict: A flood damages the village and destroys crops.
Solution: Max organizes rescue efforts and repairs irrigation systems.

Story:
Max the robot lived in a farming village where he helped people with daily work. One year, heavy rains caused severe flooding. Crops were destroyed, and villagers lost hope. Max quickly created temporary shelters and repaired damaged water channels using his engineering skills. With teamwork and determination, the village recovered. The people thanked Max for proving that intelligence and compassion can work together.
